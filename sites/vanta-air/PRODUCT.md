## Platform

## Stack

Standalone static site (user chose "Standalone site"): Vite + Three.js + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis, in `sites/vanta-air/`, separate from the LBM Next.js app.

## Users

Homeowners who need heating or cooling help. Two situations: (1) something is broken or uncomfortable now and they want a tech booked fast; (2) their system is old or failing and they are weighing a replacement, a high-ticket decision.

## Product Purpose

Vanta Air Cooling & Heating is a residential HVAC company: repair, replacement and installation, and maintenance. The site exists to turn visitors into booked service calls and replacement estimate requests.

Primary action: Book service online. Second action: Request a free estimate on a new system.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the owner:
- Same-day and 24/7 service.
- Upfront flat-rate pricing (price quoted before work starts).
- Licensed, with a parts warranty. Exact license number and warranty terms: OPEN, owner to supply. Do not state durations.
- Maintenance membership plan. Plan name, price and perks: OPEN, owner to supply.

## Product Principles

1. Speed to booked call beats everything. The booking path is never more than one tap away.
2. Price clarity is the trust lever: say "upfront flat-rate" plainly, never vague.
3. Premium, not flashy: craft earns trust for a high-ticket replacement.
4. Only confirmed claims. Placeholders over invention.
