# Directions

Pick exactly one per build. Each direction lists sites from the study that showed it, so you can check the pattern instead of guessing. Gallery names in Browse lines are Designeer's (see sources.md); Httpster is the one exception. Every font below is free and was confirmed on Fontshare (fontshare.com) or Fontsource (fontsource.org) on 2026-10-29. Hex values marked "measured" were read from computed styles on the named site; others are starting points.

---

## 1. Poster grotesk

Heavy or condensed display type at 5 to 7x body, set tight, with nothing decorative around it.

- **Seen on:** Tachyon (SEESAW), Grafik (Minimal Gallery), Spectrum.Life (Supahero), Eddie (Supahero), 13g (Supahero), Voice Tag Gods (One Page Love), DG Cinema (Supahero)
- **Type:** condensed or heavy grotesk display at 5 to 7x body (measured on the sites above: 4.7 to 7.2x), line height 0.7 to 0.95, tracking 0 to -0.05em, often uppercase. Text face is a plain grotesk or a mono at 14 to 16px.
  - Display: Anton, Bebas Neue, Oswald, Barlow Condensed (Fontsource); Clash Display, Cabinet Grotesk, Tanker (Fontshare)
  - Text: Switzer, General Sans, Satoshi (Fontshare); Hanken Grotesk, Schibsted Grotesk (Fontsource)
  - Labels: Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono (Fontsource); Azeret Mono (Fontshare)
- **Palette:** one deep field colour plus cream plus one loud accent. Tachyon measured: field #510000, panel #FAF7EF, accent #FFB2A9. Or near-black #131313 (Tequs, measured) plus white plus one signal colour.
- **Imagery:** real people at full bleed in black and white or warm grade (Grafik, Voice Tag Gods), or one graphic system (Tachyon's shards).
- **Suits:** recruiting pages, events, sales teams, competitions, sports, launches, anything built to rally a team.
- **Browse:** Supahero, Httpster (not on Designeer; filters: Typographic, Bold), One Page Love (Bold, Typographic), Minimal Gallery

## 2. Editorial type-led

The headline is a serif at weight 300 to 400, set large, with one or two other elements on the screen.

- **Seen on:** Harish (Curated Design, Dark Mode Design), Runner (SEESAW), Zed (Supahero), Intent (SEESAW), Omeigo (Dark Mode Design), Eric Jordan (Supahero)
- **Type:** serif display at weight 300 to 400, 4 to 8x body (Omeigo goes to 14x), line height 0.9 to 1.25, italics for one emphasised word. Sans or mono body at 14 to 18px.
  - Display: Instrument Serif, Fraunces, Newsreader, Playfair Display, EB Garamond (Fontsource); Gambetta, Zodiak, Erode, Sentient, Boska (Fontshare)
  - Text: Instrument Sans, Geist, Source Serif 4 (Fontsource); Switzer, Satoshi (Fontshare)
- **Palette:** one field colour, one text colour, one accent used on a single word. Harish measured: #0A0C09 field, cream text, green italic accent. Runner measured: #53291E field.
- **Imagery:** little or none. Whitespace and type carry it.
- **Suits:** founders' personal brands, coaching, consultancies, high-ticket services, long-form pages.
- **Browse:** Curated Design, Minimal Gallery, SEESAW, Httpster (not on Designeer; filters: Typographic, Minimal)

## 3. Product-led SaaS

The product UI is the hero image, filled with real data. The headline is short; the screenshot does the persuading.

- **Seen on:** Causal (Minimal Gallery), Better Stack (Supahero), Zed (Supahero), Gaia (SEESAW), Wope (Supahero), AskAiRank (Curated Design), Autorouter (SEESAW)
- **Type:** grotesk display at weight 400 to 700, 3.5 to 6x body, tracking 0 to -0.035em; body 14 to 16px; mono for numbers and code. A serif headline also works here (AskAiRank, Zed).
  - Display and text: Geist, Inter (tuned), Instrument Sans, DM Sans, Manrope, Bricolage Grotesque (Fontsource); General Sans, Satoshi, Supreme (Fontshare)
  - Data: Geist Mono, IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono (Fontsource)
- **Palette:** near-white (#F9F9F9 on Causal, measured) or near-black, greys, one brand accent on the primary CTA only.
- **Imagery:** the real product with specific content: names, numbers, timestamps. Never a placeholder dashboard.
- **Suits:** SaaS, internal tools, dashboards, CRM and pipeline views, onboarding portals, leaderboards.
- **Browse:** SEESAW, Supahero, Curated Design, Dark Mode Design, 60fps (App Sites) for interaction detail; Saaspo for SaaS-only (blocked during this study; open it manually)

## 4. Dark cinematic

Full-bleed footage or photography under white type, one small accent, text left-aligned or anchored low.

- **Seen on:** Tequs (Minimal Gallery), Little Website Co. (Minimal Gallery), Driessen Architectuur (Minimal Gallery), Atlas card (60fps App Sites), DG Cinema (Supahero), Google Health (SEESAW)
- **Type:** grotesk at weight 400 to 700, 3 to 6x body, left-aligned or set in the lower half. Text at 14 to 16px.
  - Geist, Hanken Grotesk, Familjen Grotesk (Fontsource); Switzer, General Sans, Satoshi (Fontshare); a mono for small caps labels (Atlas uses a mono at small sizes).
- **Palette:** #131313 (Tequs, measured) or pure black, white text, greys at 60 to 70% opacity for secondary lines, one accent on the CTA (Tequs uses a single blue).
- **Imagery:** real footage of the real work or place. Needs real assets; a stock clip kills it.
- **Suits:** trades and local services with good footage (contractors, architects, gyms, restaurants), film and media, physical products over $500.
- **Browse:** Minimal Gallery, Dark Mode Design, Httpster (not on Designeer; filters: Dark, Fullscreen, Photographic), Supahero

## 5. Quiet minimal

Text first, one family, 2 or 3 sizes, at least half the first viewport empty.

- **Seen on:** Felix Haas (One Page Love), Mike's CV (60fps App Sites), Josh Puckett (One Page Love), Pixel One (SEESAW); from screenshots only: s.engineering (SEESAW), Isaac Fayemi (Minimal Gallery)
- **Type:** one family, often 2 or 3 sizes only. H1-to-body ratio can be 1 to 1.5x: the hierarchy comes from position and colour, not size. 14 to 24px.
  - Geist, Instrument Sans, Inter (tuned) (Fontsource); Switzer, Satoshi, General Sans (Fontshare)
- **Palette:** white or off-white, black text, one grey. Accent optional.
- **Imagery:** one object or none (Isaac Fayemi's single chrome head; s.engineering's single reel).
- **Suits:** studios, freelancers, portfolios, investor pages, anything where the audience already knows the name.
- **Browse:** Minimal Gallery, One Page Love (Minimal, Personal), Curated Design, Httpster (not on Designeer; filters: Minimal, Monotone)

## 6. Trust-led local service

A specific promise, a real photo of the work, the phone number visible. Built to get a call or a booking.

- **Seen on:** Driessen Architectuur (Minimal Gallery: phone number button in header), Tequs (Minimal Gallery: "Cut energy use by up to 80 percent", "Proven across Norway"), Atomicdust (Minimal Gallery: "A St. Louis agency", "Book a call"), Spectrum.Life (Supahero: one "Get in touch" CTA)
- **Type:** grotesk display at weight 400 to 500, 3 to 5x body, left-aligned. Text 14 to 16px.
  - DM Sans, Manrope, Figtree, Be Vietnam Pro, Hanken Grotesk (Fontsource); General Sans, Cabinet Grotesk, Satoshi (Fontshare)
- **Palette:** off-white or the business's real brand colour as the field, dark text, one accent on the call or book button.
- **Imagery:** the owner, the crew, the finished job, the location. Real photos, ideally shot for the site.
- **Suits:** local businesses, home services, clinics, agencies with a physical market.
- **Browse:** Minimal Gallery (tags Real estate, Architecture and interior design, Consulting, Food and drink), One Page Love (Service, Restaurant), Httpster (not on Designeer; filters: Service, Property)
