# AI slop tells

Each tell is named. If a build shows one, it reads as AI-made. Remove it or replace it with a deliberate choice from the chosen direction.

1. **Purple gradient / blue-to-pink gradient.** Any violet, indigo or blue-to-pink gradient as a background, text fill or button fill.
2. **Glow halo.** A blurred coloured glow or box-shadow behind buttons or cards.
3. **Glowing pill button.** A fully rounded button with a coloured glow or gradient border.
4. **Unscaled system type.** Inter or a system font at a single weight with no type scale (headings barely larger than body).
5. **Emoji icons.** Emoji used as feature icons, bullets or section markers.
6. **Default hero.** Centred headline, subline, two buttons side by side, product screenshot underneath.
7. **Three icon cards.** A row of three equal cards, each with an icon, a short title and two lines of text.
8. **Uniform radius and shadow.** The same large border radius and the same soft shadow on every element.
9. **Endless decorative motion.** Looping floats, pulses, gradient shifts or marquees that carry no information.
10. **Hype copy.** "Revolutionize", "supercharge", "unlock". Add more only when seen on a studied site.

## Pre-show checklist (yes = hit, fix before showing)

1. Is there a purple, indigo or blue-to-pink gradient anywhere?
2. Does any button or card have a glow or coloured blur behind it?
3. Is the type Inter or a system font at one weight, with headings barely larger than body?
4. Is any emoji standing in for an icon?
5. Is the hero centred text + two buttons + screenshot below?
6. Is there a row of three equal icon cards?
7. Do all elements share the same radius and shadow?
8. Does any copy use a word from tell 10, or anything that loops with no purpose?
